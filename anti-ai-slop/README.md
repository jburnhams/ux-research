## What is distinctive

- **The blank-page result.** "In testing, 4 of 10 apps shipped as blank pages that
  passed every code-level check", and "a blank page trivially 'doesn't look AI'
  while being useless." This is the strongest argument in the collection for
  rendering before judging, and for making an empty `#root` a hard failure.
- **Set divergence.** When producing several artefacts (screens, slides), "they
  must diverge on the primary axes: ground, display face family, accent hue, and
  stance." This is the opposite requirement to avoid-ai-design's cross-page
  consistency (X1). The two are reconciled by scope: separate products should
  diverge, and pages of one product should not.
- **Fabricated precision as a tell.** "Don't invent exact stats ('38% MoM', '3.2
  days') with no source", along with the "cute 'sample data' disclaimer" tic.
- It names the second-order kit concretely: cream or dark paper, Fraunces or
  Playfair, a terracotta, clay, ember or radish accent, a corner page counter, a
  "coloured last word" headline, mono micro-labels.
